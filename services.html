Pool Decks

A pool deck takes more abuse than almost any other concrete surface — heat, moisture, foot traffic, pool chemicals. We pour and finish pool decks built to handle all of it, and built to look great doing it.

  • Broom finish, smooth, or stamped
  • Slip-resistant texture options
  • Heat-reflective color options
  • Integrates with coping, steps, and landscaping
  • Proper drainage slope built in
Add Driveway Photo

Driveways

A properly poured driveway is one of the best investments in your home. We handle everything from excavation and subgrade prep to control joints and final finish — done right the first time.

  • Proper base prep and compaction
  • Reinforcement (rebar or wire mesh)
  • Control joints to manage cracking
  • Broom or exposed aggregate finish
  • Curb cuts and apron work

Patios

Custom concrete patios that work with your yard — not against it. We build around slopes, rocks, trees, and existing features to give you a patio that fits your space perfectly.

  • Custom shapes and sizes
  • Integrates with natural grade and landscaping
  • Smooth, broom, or stamped finishes
  • Optional border and inlay designs
  • Steps and transitions included

Stamped Concrete

Decorative patterns pressed into wet concrete to replicate the look of stone, slate, brick, or wood — at a fraction of the material cost. One of our most popular services for pool decks and patios.

  • Dozens of pattern options
  • Color integral and release options
  • Slate, flagstone, cobblestone, wood plank looks
  • Sealer applied for color protection
  • Can be combined with smooth borders

Retaining Walls

Structural concrete retaining walls that hold back soil, control erosion, and create flat, usable yard space on sloped properties. Built to last and engineered to handle the load.

  • Poured concrete or block construction
  • Drainage integrated behind wall
  • Tiered wall systems for larger grades
  • Can be finished or formed to match surroundings
  • Permits pulled as required

Steps & Stairs

Entry steps, pool steps, exterior staircases — poured clean, formed square, and finished to match or complement the surrounding concrete work.

  • Entry steps for homes and additions
  • Pool steps and landings
  • Exterior staircases with handrail provisions
  • Matching finish to adjacent surfaces
  • Nosing options for safety

Garages & Slabs

Garage floors, shed pads, workshop slabs, and concrete bases for any structure. Proper thickness, proper reinforcement, proper slope — every time.

  • Standard 4" residential garage floors
  • Thickened edges for load-bearing structures
  • Vapor barrier under slab
  • Control joints at correct intervals
  • Smooth trowel finish for easy cleaning

Footings & Foundations

Footings for decks, room additions, outbuildings, and accessory structures. We follow local code specs, set forms level, and pour to the depth the project requires.

  • Deck and pergola footings
  • Addition and outbuilding foundations
  • Continuous and spread footings
  • Rebar placement per spec
  • Same-day pour after inspection when possible
