FAQ

Questions, Answered

Can you build a patio on a sloped backyard?

Yes — sloped yards are one of the most common situations we work with in the Charlotte area. We can grade a flat patio into a slope, or build a stepped, multi-level patio that follows the grade naturally.

What's the difference between a broom finish and a stamped patio?

A broom finish is a simple textured surface, budget-friendly and slip-resistant. Stamped concrete presses a pattern (like flagstone or brick) into the surface for a decorative look at a lower cost than natural stone.

Do you install fire pits or seat walls with the patio?

We can pour a concrete base or footing for a fire pit or seat wall as part of the patio project — let us know what you're planning during the estimate walkthrough.
