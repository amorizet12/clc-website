FAQ

Questions, Answered

How thick does a garage slab need to be?

4 inches is standard for a typical residential garage. If you're planning to store heavier equipment, a lift, or an RV, we can pour thicker with added reinforcement.

Do I need a vapor barrier?

Yes, we recommend it for any enclosed slab like a garage or shop floor — it keeps ground moisture from migrating up through the concrete, which can cause issues with flooring, storage, or humidity inside the structure.

Can you pour a slab for a shed I haven't built yet?

Yes — just give us the dimensions from your shed kit or builder's plans and we'll pour a slab sized and leveled to spec so it's ready when the structure goes up.
